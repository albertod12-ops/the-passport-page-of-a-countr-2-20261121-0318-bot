The passport spread of the Kingdom of Zanvara — a country that isn't
there. A hidden highland kingdom in the Great Rift highlands, revealed
to the world in 2019, whose solar-lattice technology runs on sunstone,
a crystal found nowhere else. The holder, Amara Ndele, is a licensed
solar lattice engineer (grid lic. SL-207): her occupation, the solar
lattice endorsement, the sunstone levy stamp, the visa from
neighbouring Kessari, and a paired entry/exit travel history all agree
with that, dated after the passport was issued on 09 MAY 2023 and
before it expires on 08 MAY 2033.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, Pillow) draws the whole 1024x1024 spread to
`/output/entry.png` — paper, guilloche, emblem, data page with an ICAO
machine-readable zone (check digits computed, not typed), endorsement,
visa, eight stamps spread down the page, a grid-sync strip, and a
ghost portrait. The portrait is a photograph
generated for this entry, kept as `portrait.jpg`. No network, no
randomness: two runs produce the same bytes.
